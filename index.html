Jonathan García Fuentes

Ph.D. Student · Humboldt-University of Berlin

I am a Ph.D. student and a Research Fellow at the Zero Hunger Lab, a joint initiative of HU and IGZ. I am also an affiliated researcher at the Thaer Institute, CIDER (Cornell University), and ISDC.

About

I am a development economist, with a focus on food security, conflict, and humanitarian food assistance. My current work examines how the phase-in and phase-out, targeting, and design of humanitarian food assistance affect household well-being and community dynamics.

Before my Ph.D., I worked for the United Nations World Food Programme in the country offices of Nicaragua, Mozambique (Maputo and Cabo Delgado), and Sudan, as well as in the regional offices of Latin America and Southern Africa (Panama and Johannesburg). I have also held research assistant positions at the University of Göttingen, DIW Berlin, and Leibniz University Hannover.

My doctoral research is supervised by Prof. Tilman Brück and Prof. Andrea Guariso.
