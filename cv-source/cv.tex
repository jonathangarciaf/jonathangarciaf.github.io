\vspace{4pt}
Development economist and Ph.D.\ candidate specializing in impact evaluation 
and the optimization of humanitarian interventions in fragile settings. 
My research uses machine learning and large-scale RCTs to test program design 
variations in the DRC and Lebanon. 
I have over \textbf{10 years} of research and operational experience with a primary 
focus on quantitative methods. I have led large-scale surveys and targeting 
exercises in some of the world's largest humanitarian operations, and advised 
country operations and governments across Nicaragua, Sudan, Mozambique, and 
the Latin America and Southern Africa regions.
